## 📜 Plot Summary (Spoiler Alert!)

When an unexpected enemy emerges that threatens global safety and security, Nick Fury, Director of the international peacekeeping agency S.H.I.E.L.D., is forced to bring together a team to pull the world back from the brink of disaster.

> **Quote:** *"We have a Hulk."* - Tony Stark

The team faces Loki, Thor's adoptive brother, who gains control of the Tesseract and attempts to conquer Earth with the help of the alien race known as the Chitauri. After initial internal conflicts, the heroes unite to defend New York City in a climactic battle.
